Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor of Political Science at New York University Shanghai. I am also affiliated with the Center for Global Asia and the NYU Sovereign Debt Network.

My research sits at the intersection of international political economy, international organizations, public finance, and global governance. I study how states bargain, cooperate, and compete over the rules and institutions that govern development finance, taxation, and multinational economic activity. Substantively, my work examines multilateral development banks, the politics of development finance, international tax avoidance, and the institutional foundations of global economic governance.

My work has been published or is forthcoming in International Organization, The Review of International Organizations, Review of International Political Economy, and Public Administration.

I received my PhD in Politics from Princeton University in 2023, where I was affiliated with the Niehaus Center for Globalization and Governance. Before joining NYU Shanghai, I was a Postgraduate Research Associate and Lecturer in Princeton's Politics Department. I received my B.A. and M.A. from Fudan University.
